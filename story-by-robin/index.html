Story by Robin

May 19, 2021

We were just getting to know John Moates over this past year but he is an easy person to get to know and like right away. The first time we came into their home he was quick to make us feel at home and part of the group. It was obvious how much he loved Ryane and his daughters and that he was a man of God who loved life and loved people. Someone who lived life to the fullest and did not take life for granted. I’ve loved reading the other stories about John. This world is most definitely a better place because of John Moates. I know one day we will all be rejoicing and praising the Lord together and that this is exactly what he is doing in the presence of the Lord right this moment! We love you and your family Ryane and we are here for you friend.

Sent from my iPhone
