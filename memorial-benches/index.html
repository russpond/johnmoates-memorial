Memorial Benches

Would you like to visit one of John’s Memorial Benches?

Trinity River Bench

This one is by the Trinity River in downtown Fort Worth. It is in a public area and can be visited at any time.  Park at the location in this link, hike up over the levee and it is on the other side.

https://maps.app.goo.gl/mFa9cJ1kr5ULyfWX6



Euless Family Life Center Bench

This one is inside the Euless Family Life Center, by the racquetball courts.  John played racquetball here with his buddies, 3 days a week for 10 years!  Location and hours of operation can be found at this link.

https://www.eulesstx.gov/recreation/euless-family-life-center

Thanks to Liz Johnson, Recreation Superintendent at Euless Parks and Community Services, we now have a plaque to commemorate John’s bench at the Euless Family Life Center.  What a wonderful tribute to our friend who we miss every time we play.  The bench is located next to the racquetball courts.
